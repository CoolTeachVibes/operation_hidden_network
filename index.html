Evaluator's Toolkit

Watch the action

Observation

Focus: real-time behavior.

Evidence: participation, interaction, interruptions, non-verbal cues, decision-making.

Strength: direct evidence of what people actually do.

Limitation: behavior may not explain why it occurs.

Example: “Who speaks, who interrupts, and how are decisions made?”

Map the structure

Sociogram

Focus: social structure.

Evidence: preferences, affiliations, clusters, central members, bridges, weak connections.

Strength: makes hidden structures visible.

Limitation: structure does not automatically explain motive.

Example: “Who collaborates with whom most often?”

Ask about the why

Interview

Focus: individual experience and interpretation.

Evidence: perceptions, concerns, motivations, goals, explanations.

Strength: provides depth and perspective.

Limitation: self-report may not perfectly match actual behavior.

Example: “What makes it easy or difficult for you to participate?”

OBSERVE = ACTION · SOCIOGRAM = STRUCTURE · INTERVIEW = WHY / PERSPECTIVE
